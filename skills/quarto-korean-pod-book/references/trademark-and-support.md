# Korean trademark and small-business support

Operational checklist only; obtain a Korean patent attorney for refusal risk, conflicts, licensing, assignment, or enforcement.

## Search and define

Search KIPRIS for exact Korean, spacing variants, English, transliteration, pronunciation, meaning, and composite logos. Inspect pending/registered status, owner, and designated goods/services.

- KIPRIS: https://www.kipris.or.kr/
- KIPO/IP Office: https://www.kipo.go.kr/

Record query, date, classes, results, and conflict analysis. No exact match is not a guarantee.

Confirm owner identity/address, word versus logo mark, actual/planned goods/services, official Nice classes/designated wording, and domestic/international plan. Publishing often raises class 16 (printed books), 41 (publishing/education), and sometimes 9 (downloadable electronic publications); these are examples, not automatic filings.

## Check support before self-filing

- Regional IP Center: https://pms.ripc.org/www/main.do
- Bizinfo: https://www.bizinfo.go.kr/

Some programs exclude applications filed before selection. Capture the full current-year notice and verify status/budget, small-business and regional eligibility, excluded industries, mark quantity/type, self-pay, VAT, official/attorney fees, prior filing/beneficiary exclusions, service provider, ownership, evidence, and deadline.

Regional 2026 notices demonstrate that support may exist until budget exhaustion, not that it is universal. Example: https://www.bizinfo.go.kr/sii/siia/selectSIIA200Detail.do?pblancId=PBLN_000000000119045

Do not assume general fee reductions cover trademarks. Check the exact program and fee table.

## File and prosecute

Patent-ro generally requires a patent customer number, certificate registration, software/form preparation, online submission, and result confirmation.

- Patent-ro: https://www.patent.go.kr/
- Domestic filing: https://www.patent.go.kr/smart/jsp/kiponet/ma/websolution/OnlineIndex.do
- Tutorial: https://www.patent.go.kr/smart/jsp/ka/navigation/NaviStepC.do
- Current fees: https://kporc.kipo.go.kr/smart/jsp/ka/menu/fee/main/FeeMain01.do

Never hardcode fees. Archive filing form, specimen, goods list, receipt, application number, payment, and deadlines. Monitor office actions, publication/opposition, registration decision, and registration payment.

Before actual registration, never print `®`, `등록상표`, or `상표등록 완료`. Plain brand use such as `스칼라브릿지` is not a registration claim.

## Evidence and renewal

Preserve dated mark sources, print-ready cover and checksum, POD upload/order, invoices/delivery, physical cover/spine photos, dated product/advertising captures, and contracts.

Korean trademark registration is generally protected for 10 years and renewable in 10-year periods. Confirm the certificate and current official procedure: https://www.kipo.go.kr/ko/kpoContentView.do?menuCd=SCD0200209

Keep English logo and Korean word mark as separate editable elements when using both; this preserves design flexibility and clearer evidence of actual use.
