# End-to-end workflow

## 0. Publishing brief

Record working title, reader, problem, promise, differentiator, comparable books, length, print/color format, budget, deadline, author/publisher identity, sales channels, and success criterion.

## 1. Architecture and sample

1. Convert the promise into 3–5 parts and chapter-level reader questions.
2. Give each chapter a job: question, evidence, counterargument, decision, takeaway.
3. Draft one representative chapter and test it with target readers.
4. Freeze house rules for Korean spacing, transliteration, names, units, numbers, citations, captions, and AI-generated material.

## 2. Rights and evidence

Maintain an asset ledger with ID, path, creator, source, license/permission, credit, modification, resolution, and status. Preserve interview consent, quotation permissions, font licenses, and commissioned-work contracts. Give changing facts an access date.

If generative AI is used, record tool, date, purpose, human verification, and required platform disclosure. Never treat generated citations as verified.

## 3. Editing order

1. Developmental edit: thesis, reader journey, omissions, repetition.
2. Technical/fact review: claims, calculations, sources, names, dates.
3. Sensitivity/legal/rights review as relevant.
4. Copyedit: grammar, consistency, terminology, citations.
5. Typeset proofread: line breaks, widows/orphans, tables, figures, running heads, TOC, index.

Do not polish sentences before structure or finalize the spine before pagination.

## 4. Administration

Common sequence:

1. Complete publisher notification/registration with the competent local authority if required.
2. Align business registration and settlement identity.
3. Create a publisher account in the National Library ISBN system; request distinct ISBNs for formats when required.
4. Create the POD partner account and verify settlement documents.
5. Prepare title, subtitle, author, publisher, date, price, category, description, keywords, bio, TOC, cover, and sample.

Official starting points:

- National Library ISBN/ISSN: https://www.nl.go.kr/seoji/
- Kyobo Purple notices: https://store.kyobobook.co.kr/pod/notice
- Kyobo Purple introduction: https://store.kyobobook.co.kr/pod/introduce

## 5. Proof and release

Inspect the physical proof for title/author/publisher/ISBN/barcode, cover color and folds, spine centering, finish, gutter, binding, page sequence, blank pages, glyph substitution, thin rules, image detail, table readability, and QR/links.

Log page, severity, source, correction, and verification. Regenerate instead of patching the PDF. Archive the approved checksums.

## 6. Post-publication

Archive source, PDFs, metadata, ISBN notice, upload receipt, proof photos, invoices, and product-page capture. Track errata and create deliberate versions without overwriting evidence.
